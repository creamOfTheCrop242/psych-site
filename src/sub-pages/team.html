Lisa Wolff

Our Senior counselor, Lisa Wolff has been practicing since 2015 and is a graduate of Loyola University. She earned a Master’s of Science in Mental Health and Pastoral Counseling and is a Licensed mental health counselor for the state of Maryland. She has a passion for helping to bring hope, wholeness, and healing to those she treats.
