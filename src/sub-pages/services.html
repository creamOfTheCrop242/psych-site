PLANNING YOUR FIRST VISIT

Questions before you begin?

Where is the practice located?

Our office is at 2138 Priest Bridge Ct., Suite 1, Crofton, MD 21114. Call (667) 239-8348 if you need help finding us.

Are virtual appointments available?

Yes. Our team offers virtual appointments, and some counselors also offer in-person sessions. Visit our team page to explore each counselor’s appointment options.

How do I schedule an appointment?

Use the Book an appointment link to open our scheduling portal, or call (667) 239-8348 to speak with the practice.

Do you accept insurance?

Our finances page lists accepted insurance and session fees. Contact the practice and your insurer to confirm coverage and any out-of-pocket costs before your appointment.

Is Christian counseling available?

Christian spiritual integration is available if you want it included in your counseling. Talk with your counselor about your preferences and goals.
